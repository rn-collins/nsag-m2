AI Legal Navigation

12 questions  ·  6 dimensions  ·  8–10 minutes

AI tools are now embedded in legal contexts that affect millions — risk assessment instruments in bail, benefits eligibility algorithms, predictive policing systems. Most of the people affected don't know these tools are used. Most organizations using them can't explain how they work.

Question 1 of 12

This assessment scores your organization across six dimensions. Each question uses a five-point scale. Your tier report is generated immediately after you submit your email — no waiting, no sales call required.

For: Court innovation offices · Legal aid tech teams · Public defender AI tools · Access-to-justice organizations

Q1 of 12
AI Tool Transparency & Explainability

Can your organization produce a plain-language explanation of how each AI tool used in legal decision-making works — including what data it uses and what it predicts or recommends?

Q2 of 12
AI Tool Transparency & Explainability

Does your organization maintain an up-to-date inventory of all AI tools that influence legal decisions, including those operated by third-party vendors or government agencies?

Q3 of 12
Bias Audit & Demographic Impact

Has each AI tool in use at your organization been independently audited for demographic disparities in outcomes — including race, gender, age, language, and disability status?

Q4 of 12
Bias Audit & Demographic Impact

Does your organization review AI tool performance data by demographic category at regular intervals — and is there a defined disparity threshold that would trigger suspension or replacement of a tool?

Q5 of 12
Human Oversight & Override Protocols

Does your organization have a formal protocol for human review of AI-generated recommendations before they influence a legal decision affecting a specific individual?

Q6 of 12
Human Oversight & Override Protocols

Can a staff member override an AI recommendation without documentation burden, disciplinary risk, or workflow friction that effectively coerces deference to the algorithm?

Q7 of 12
Plain-Language Communication to Affected Parties

Are individuals informed when an AI tool has contributed to a legal decision affecting them — in plain language, before the decision is finalized?

Q8 of 12
Plain-Language Communication to Affected Parties

Do affected individuals have a meaningful opportunity to contest AI-generated assessments, and are they informed of this right at the point of impact?

Q9 of 12
Accountability & Error Correction

Does your organization have a documented process for identifying, reporting, and correcting errors produced by AI tools — including a timeline for response and a mechanism for notifying affected individuals?

Q10 of 12
Accountability & Error Correction

When an AI tool produces an outcome later found to be incorrect or biased, does your organization have a formal process for remediation of harm to affected individuals?

Q11 of 12
Community Input & Governance Structures

Does your organization have a formal mechanism for community members — particularly those most affected by AI-assisted legal decisions — to provide input on AI tool adoption, design, and discontinuation?

Q12 of 12
Community Input & Governance Structures

Is there a governance body or process with genuine decision-making authority over AI tool use in your organization that includes perspectives beyond legal and technical staff?
